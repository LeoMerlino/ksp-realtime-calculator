KSP counts Universal Time (UT) in seconds since Year 1, Day 1, 00:00:00. The in-game clock can be toggled between Kerbin Time (6 h days, 426 d years) and Earth Time (24 h days, 365 d years); the underlying seconds are identical.

Notes

KSP has no real-world epoch, so mapping a Kerbin date to an Earth calendar date requires choosing an arbitrary Earth date for UT = 0. Adjust the Epoch field as needed. Mission Elapsed Time (MET) uses the same Kerbin seconds since launch.
